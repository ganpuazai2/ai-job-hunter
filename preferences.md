1. AI solutions / implementation / pre-sales (solutions consultant, AI adoption, customer-facing AI roles)
2. AI partnerships / business development at AI or AI-adjacent companies
3. AI product / project (project manager, associate product manager, product operations)
4. AI automation / operations

Fintech and payments companies working on AI are a plus, since my background is there.

## Pay
- At least S$6,000/month for directions 1 to 3.
- At least S$4,500/month for direction 4 (AI automation / ops), as I'm moving into it.
- When a posting gives no salary, don't penalise it; judge seniority instead.

## Location
Singapore, or remote roles open to someone based in Singapore. Roles based in another country (e.g. Vietnam, Philippines, Australia only) are a poor fit unless Singapore is clearly an option.

## Red flags
- Needs hands-on software engineering, ML engineering or a computer science degree as a hard requirement.
- Director/VP level needing 10+ years, or entry-level/internship roles.
- Pure cold-calling sales with no partnership or solutions element.
- Short contracts (under 12 months) unless the pay is clearly above range.
